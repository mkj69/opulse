# Worlds

<div class="ow-page" markdown>

<p class="ow-lead" markdown>Each world starts with a chosen set of [atoms](../glossary.md), or operations computed directly. Explore the examples here, or [build your own](../guide/atoms.md). Shapes such as ◆₁ name operators but do not reveal their rules ([notation](../guide/notation.md)).</p>

In each Challenge tab, puzzle 6 shows examples of a hidden rule. Choose the output for a new input, then reveal the rule and its explanation.

Each world lists every atom with its signature, meaning, and a rendered example above the tabs. The Operators tab starts with a curated selection. Use **Show all grown operators** to inspect the complete, unfiltered output of the seeded generator.

The [curation report](curation-report.md) lists each world's rejection counts and featured rules.
